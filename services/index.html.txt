<!-- ============ SECTION 3: PROBLEMS ============ -->
<section class="section" id="problems">
  <div class="wrap">
    <div class="section-head">
      <span class="eyebrow">Local Expertise</span>
      <h2>Chicago Tree Problems We Help Solve</h2>
      <p>Chicagoland's tree stock faces a specific set of regional threats. Here's what our arborists diagnose and
      treat most often.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="problem-grid">
      <div class="problem-card">
        <div class="icon">🐛</div>
        <h3>Emerald Ash Borer</h3>
        <p>This invasive beetle has devastated ash trees across Chicagoland. Early detection and treatment can save
        a tree; advanced infestations usually require removal before the tree becomes hazardous.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="problem-card">
        <div class="icon">🍂</div>
        <h3>Dutch Elm Disease</h3>
        <p>A fungal disease spread by bark beetles that causes rapid wilting and canopy dieback in elms. Prompt
        pruning of infected limbs and fungicide treatment can slow its spread.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="problem-card">
        <div class="icon">🌳</div>
        <h3>Oak Wilt</h3>
        <p>Highly contagious among oak species and spreadable through root grafts and pruning wounds. We schedule oak
        work outside the high-risk spring-to-fall window to reduce transmission.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="problem-card">
        <div class="icon">⚠️</div>
        <h3>Silver Maple Failures</h3>
        <p>Fast-growing but structurally weak, mature silver maples are a common source of limb failure in storms.
        Structural pruning and cabling reduce the risk of sudden breakage.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="problem-card">
        <div class="icon">🌪️</div>
        <h3>Storm Damage</h3>
        <p>Chicago's exposure to derechos and high-wind events leaves broken limbs, split trunks, and leaning trees.
        We provide emergency response to secure the property fast.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="problem-card">
        <div class="icon">🪵</div>
        <h3>Tree Root Conflicts</h3>
        <p>Roots lifting sidewalks, driveways, and foundations are common in older Chicago lots. We assess root
        management options that protect the tree and the hardscape.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="problem-card">
        <div class="icon">⚡</div>
        <h3>Utility Clearance Issues</h3>
        <p>Branches encroaching on energized power lines require utility-aware pruning and, in some cases, coordination
        with utility vegetation management crews.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="problem-card">
        <div class="icon">🕰️</div>
        <h3>Aging Trees</h3>
        <p>Many Chicago parkway and yard trees were planted decades ago and are now showing structural decline. A
        tree risk assessment determines whether pruning, support, or removal is the safest path.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>

<section class="section" id="faq">
  <div class="wrap">
    <div class="section-head">
      <span class="eyebrow">Common Questions</span>
      <h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
    </div>
    <div class="faq-list">
      <details class="faq-item" open>
        <summary>What tree services does Prime Tree Care provide?</summary>
        <div class="a">Tree health inspection and disease treatment, tree and shrub fertilization, tree trimming and pruning, tree planting and transplanting, tree removal, stump grinding, storm prep and tree support systems, emergency storm response, and land or lot clearing across Chicago and surrounding suburbs.</div>
      </details>
      <details class="faq-item">
        <summary>How much does tree service cost in Chicago?</summary>
        <div class="a">Pricing depends on tree size, species, condition, accessibility, and proximity to structures or power lines. Routine pruning is typically less costly than a full removal, and hazardous or storm-damaged trees near utility lines cost more due to added safety precautions. See our <a href="/tree-removal-cost-chicago/">tree removal cost guide</a> and <a href="/tree-trimming-cost-chicago/">trimming cost guide</a> for typical ranges, or request a free on-site estimate for an exact price.</div>
      </details>
      <details class="faq-item">
        <summary>When should trees be pruned in Chicago?</summary>
        <div class="a">Most trees do best with dormant-season pruning in late winter, which limits stress and disease exposure. Oaks specifically should avoid pruning between April and October to reduce oak wilt risk. Dead or hazardous limbs should be addressed immediately, regardless of season.</div>
      </details>
      <details class="faq-item">
        <summary>How do I know if a tree is hazardous?</summary>
        <div class="a">Watch for large dead branches, fungus or mushrooms at the base, deep trunk cracks, a new or worsening lean, lifting roots, and thinning or discolored canopy. An ISA-certified arborist can perform a full tree risk assessment to confirm the right next step.</div>
      </details>
      <details class="faq-item">
        <summary>Can you help after storm damage?</summary>
        <div class="a">Yes — we provide 24/7 emergency storm response across Chicago and surrounding suburbs, including downed limb removal and leaning tree stabilization, with safe procedures around energized power lines.</div>
      </details>
      <details class="faq-item">
        <summary>Do I need a permit to remove a tree?</summary>
        <div class="a">Many Chicago neighborhoods regulate parkway trees through the Bureau of Forestry (reachable via CHI311), and suburb-specific rules vary for private property. Read our <a href="/do-i-need-a-permit-to-remove-a-tree-chicago/">permit guide</a> or ask us to confirm requirements for your address before work begins.</div>
      </details>
      <details class="faq-item">
        <summary>What is the difference between pruning and tree removal?</summary>
        <div class="a">Pruning selectively removes specific branches to improve health, structure, or safety while keeping the tree alive. Removal takes the entire tree down and is typically reserved for trees that are dead, structurally unsound, severely diseased, or hazardous.</div>
      </details>
      <details class="faq-item">
        <summary>Can an arborist diagnose tree diseases?</summary>
        <div class="a">Yes. Our ISA-certified arborists identify common regional issues such as <a href="/emerald-ash-borer-chicago/">Emerald Ash Borer</a> and <a href="/dutch-elm-disease-chicago/">Dutch Elm Disease</a> through visual inspection and diagnostic testing, then recommend treatment, pruning, or removal.</div>
      </details>
