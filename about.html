About Prime Tree Care

Chicago-Grown Tree Care, Built on Certification and Honesty

We're a locally owned tree care company built specifically around the problems Chicago's trees actually face — not a national franchise running the same playbook everywhere it goes.

Our Story

Built for Chicago's Trees, Not a Generic Playbook

Prime Tree Care started with a simple frustration: too many Chicago homeowners were getting the same cut-and-paste tree service that works fine in the suburbs of Texas or Florida, but doesn't account for what's actually happening here — heavy clay soil, an ash canopy under real pressure from emerald ash borer, and storm seasons that have gotten noticeably more severe in recent years.

We built our crew, our equipment, and our diagnostic approach around those specific local conditions. Every arborist on our team is ISA-certified, and every estimate is delivered by someone who has actually inspected your tree in person — not a call-center script reading back a generic price range.

Today, we serve homeowners, property managers, and commercial clients across Chicago's Northwest Side and throughout Cook County, with the same commitment we started with: certified expertise, honest pricing, and work that's done right the first time.

What We Stand For

The Principles Behind Every Job

These aren't marketing lines — they're how we actually operate, from the first phone call to the final cleanup.

Certified Expertise

Every diagnosis and cut is led by an ISA-certified arborist — not a subcontracted crew learning on the job.

Total Transparency

Written, itemized estimates before we start. No vague verbal quotes, no surprise fees once the crew arrives.

Safety First, Always

Crane and bucket-truck access when a job calls for it. We never cut corners near structures or power lines.

Genuinely Local

We know Chicago's soil, species, storm patterns, and parkway-tree rules because we work here every single day.

Tree Health First

Removal is a last resort, not a default. We'll always tell you when treatment or pruning can save a tree.

Reliable Response

Same-day quotes for most requests, and 24/7 crews on call for storm damage and genuine emergencies.
