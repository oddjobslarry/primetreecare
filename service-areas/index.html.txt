    <div>
      <span class="eyebrow">Local Expertise</span>
      <h2 style="margin-top:14px;">Why Local Tree Care Knowledge Matters in Chicago</h2>
      <p style="margin-top:18px;">
        Tree care in Chicago is not a one-size-fits-all job. The region's dense clay soils compact easily under
        foot traffic and construction, restricting root growth and leaving trees more vulnerable to drought stress
        and storm-related failure. Layer on a long-running Emerald Ash Borer infestation, recurring Dutch Elm
        Disease outbreaks, and oak wilt risk in warmer months, and it becomes clear why generic "trim and remove"
        service isn't enough — a company needs to understand which threats are active in which part of the city,
        and when.
      </p>
      <p style="margin-top:14px;">
        Chicago's exposure to derecho-strength straight-line winds and heavy lake-effect snow and ice loading also
        changes how trees should be pruned and supported. A structurally weak silver maple that survives a mild
        summer can fail catastrophically in a high-wind event, and heavy wet snow regularly snaps limbs that weren't
        properly thinned the season before.
      </p>
      <p style="margin-top:14px;">
        Tree care needs also shift by geography. Dense city neighborhoods often have mature parkway trees planted
        decades ago, tight lot lines, and closer proximity to overhead utility lines — all of which require
        utility-aware pruning and coordination with the <strong>Bureau of Forestry</strong> (residents can report
        parkway tree issues through <strong>CHI311</strong>). Older inner-ring suburbs like Oak Park and Evanston
        tend to carry a similarly mature canopy with established root systems that need protecting during
        construction or hardscaping. Newer western suburbs such as Aurora, West Chicago, and Schaumburg, by contrast,
        often have younger trees that benefit most from structural training pruning and species diversification to
        avoid repeating the ash-monoculture mistakes of the past.
      </p>
      <p style="margin-top:14px;">
        A proper <strong>tree risk assessment</strong> accounts for all of this — species, soil, sun exposure, wind
        corridor, proximity to structures and utility lines, and the specific regulations that apply to that
        property. That's the standard every Prime Tree Care evaluation is built on.
      </p>
    </div>
    <div class="stat-card">

    <div class="problem-grid">
      <div class="problem-card">

        <h3>Emerald Ash Borer</h3>
        <p>This invasive beetle continues to devastate ash trees across Chicagoland. Early detection allows for
        treatment; advanced infestations typically require removal.</p>
        <a class="link" href="/services/tree-health-inspection-treatment/">Related: Health Inspection →</a>
      </div>
      <div class="problem-card">
        <div class="icon">🍂</div>
        <h3>Dutch Elm Disease</h3>
        <p>A fungal disease spread by bark beetles that causes rapid canopy dieback in elms, especially common in
        suburbs like Elmhurst with dense elm plantings.</p>
        <a class="link" href="/services/tree-trimming-pruning/">Related: Pruning →</a>
      </div>
      <div class="problem-card">
        <div class="icon">⚠️</div>
        <h3>Silver Maple Failure</h3>
        <p>Fast-growing but structurally weak, mature silver maples are a leading cause of storm-related limb
        failure throughout our service area.</p>
        <a class="link" href="/services/storm-prep-tree-support-systems/">Related: Support Systems →</a>
      </div>
      <div class="problem-card">
        <div class="icon">🌪️</div>
        <h3>Storm-Damaged Trees</h3>
        <p>Derecho-strength winds and heavy snow loading regularly cause broken limbs, split trunks, and leaning
        trees across Chicago and the suburbs.</p>
        <a class="link" href="/services/emergency-tree-removal/">Related: Emergency Service →</a>
      </div>
      <div class="problem-card">
        <div class="icon">🪵</div>
        <h3>Root Damage Near Sidewalks</h3>
        <p>Mature trees in older neighborhoods frequently lift sidewalks and driveways as roots search for space in
        compacted soil.</p>
        <a class="link" href="/services/tree-shrub-fertilization/">Related: Root-Zone Care →</a>
      </div>
      <div class="problem-card">
        <div class="icon">🧱</div>
        <h3>Clay Soil Stress</h3>
        <p>Chicagoland's heavy clay soils compact easily and drain poorly, stressing root systems and limiting a
        tree's ability to recover from injury or drought.</p>
        <a class="link" href="/services/tree-shrub-fertilization/">Related: Fertilization →</a>
      </div>
      <div class="problem-card">
        <div class="icon">🌳</div>
        <h3>Oak Wilt Risk</h3>
        <p>Highly contagious among oaks, oak wilt spreads through root grafts and fresh pruning wounds, making
        pruning timing critical in warmer months.</p>
        <a class="link" href="/services/tree-trimming-pruning/">Related: Pruning →</a>
      </div>
